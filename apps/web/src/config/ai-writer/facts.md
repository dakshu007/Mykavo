# MyKavo AI Keywords & Blog Writer: facts for page copy

Every claim on the AI Writer pages must be true to this sheet. If it is not here, do not claim it.

## What it is
- Part of MyKavo (https://mykavo.app), in the dashboard under "AI Keywords". MyKavo is also website monitoring; the writer is the growth half.
- Flow: 1) Learns the business: reads the website and fills in a short profile (business, audience, services, market, language) that the user confirms. 2) Finds what to write: a keyword report. 3) Writes the post in one click. 4) The user edits it in MyKavo's editor, then copies it to the site or saves it as a draft in WordPress.

## Where keywords come from (the "built to rank" core)
- The site's own Google Search Console data (optional, but it makes results better), including "striking distance" searches the site already ranks for in positions 8 to 20.
- Google search suggestions (autocomplete): real searches people type.
- Today's live Google results for the keyword: what page one shows, used to judge competition and intent.
- Keywords are ranked by what to write first.
- Popularity is a 1 to 5 estimate from Google autocomplete, NOT a monthly search volume. MyKavo does not show search volume. Recommend Google Keyword Planner if someone needs volume.
- Free plan shows the top 10 keywords per report; paid plans show all.

## What one post contains
- A title, a URL slug, a meta description, the full article (with H2/H3 structure), an FAQ section, JSON-LD structured data, and review notes (what to check and where to add your own experience).
- 1 free rewrite per post.
- It stays a draft. MyKavo never publishes anything to a site.

## WordPress
- Free MyKavo WordPress plugin (on WordPress.org: https://wordpress.org/plugins/mykavo/).
- "Save as draft in WordPress": MyKavo queues the post and the plugin collects it when someone opens WP Admin and every 15 minutes in the background. It creates a WordPress draft (or updates the draft it made before while it is still a draft). It never publishes. Needs plugin version 1.3.2 or newer.
- No automatic publishing, no scheduling of posts into WordPress.
- Other sites (Shopify, Webflow, Wix, custom): copy the post from the editor.

## What it does NOT do (say so when relevant)
- No rank tracking, no backlink analysis, no keyword database with search volumes.
- No guarantee of rankings. No tool can guarantee rankings.
- No automatic publishing. No content-score editor like Surfer's.
- Not a general chat writing assistant for emails, ads or social posts. It writes SEO blog posts for your own site.

## What Google says (safe to state)
- Google rewards helpful, original content however it is produced, AI included.
- Using automation mainly to manipulate rankings (mass-producing low-value pages) is against Google's spam policies ("scaled content abuse").
- So the work that matters: target a real search, match what page one shows, add original experience, check facts.

## Voice and rules
- Plain, confident, specific. Short sentences. US English.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. Use commas, colons, or "to" for ranges (e.g. "8 to 20").
- No fake testimonials, no fake numbers, no "trusted by X teams", no "#1".
- "Built to rank" and "writes for searches you can actually win" are fine. "Guaranteed rankings" or "it ranks" as a promise are not.
- Be honest where a competitor is the better choice.
