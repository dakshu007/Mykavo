# MyKavo — 1:40 Promo Video Brief

**Deliverable:** a 100-second motion-graphics video. No live footage, no voice
actor required (narration can be TTS). Everything below is factual and
verified against the product — do not add features, numbers or claims that are
not on this page.

---

## 1. The product in one line

> **MyKavo** monitors websites for meaningful change, and tells you what broke
> before your client does.

**Tagline (use verbatim, it closes the video):**
> Know what changed. Fix what matters.

---

## 2. The problem

Websites break silently.

A plugin updates. A deploy ships. Someone edits a page. And the site keeps
returning **HTTP 200** — perfectly healthy, as far as any uptime monitor is
concerned — while the checkout button has vanished, the pricing page has picked
up a `noindex` tag, or the analytics script has stopped loading.

Nothing alerts. Nothing errors. **Silence reads as "everything is fine."**

The agency finds out three weeks later, when the client calls, angry — and by
then the revenue, the rankings or the conversion data are already gone.

**The gap:** uptime tools answer *"did the server respond?"*. Nobody was
answering *"is the page still right?"*

---

## 3. What MyKavo does

You approve what "correct" looks like — a **baseline**. MyKavo then re-scans on
a schedule, compares every element against that approved state, scores what it
finds by severity, and shows you the before and after side by side.

Detection is **deterministic** — hashes, pixel comparison and value diffs. No AI
guesswork, no false confidence.

### The workflow (four beats — good for an animated sequence)

1. **Add a site** → MyKavo discovers your pages
2. **Approve a baseline** → this is "correct"
3. **It watches** → automatic re-scans, every page compared
4. **You get told** → one grouped alert, severity-ranked, with evidence

---

## 4. What it catches — the eight categories

| Category | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Is the page still up? *(200 → 404 is CRITICAL)* |
| **Visual** | Does it still look right? *(screenshot diff)* |
| **SEO** | Did the title, meta, canonical or robots tag change? *(index → noindex is CRITICAL)* |
| **Content** | Did the text or structure change? |
| **Links** | Did internal links break? |
| **Scripts** | Did analytics, tag manager or the payment script disappear? |
| **Performance** | Is the page heavier or slower? |
| **Conversion** | **Is the signup button still there?** |

Severity ladder, lowest to highest — use as an on-screen visual:
`INFO → LOW → MEDIUM → HIGH → CRITICAL`

## 5. Beyond change monitoring

- **Site Audit** — a full technical crawl: **89 checks across 22 categories**,
  up to 1,500 pages. Every issue explains what it is, why it matters, and which
  URLs it was found on.
- **Search Console integration** — syncs clicks, impressions and positions, then
  correlates them with audit findings into **Priority Opportunities**: pages
  already earning traffic that carry a fixable technical problem.
- **Uptime & SSL** — five-minute health checks and certificate expiry warnings.
- **Client reports** — white-label, scheduled, ready to forward.
- **Six free tools** — no signup required.

## 8. Brand — use these exact values

### Colours
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Canvas (page background) | `#FBFAF3` warm paper |
| Panel (alternate band) | `#F3F1E6` |
| Cards | `#FFFFFF` |
| Ink (text + dark bands) | `#151515` |
| **Gold — the single accent** | `#FFD400` |
| Gold tint | `#FFF3B0` |
| Secondary text (light bg) | `#6B6B60` |
| Text on dark bands | `#E9EBDF` |
| Secondary on dark | `#9C9E93` |

**Gold is the only accent colour.** Use it sparingly, for emphasis and the CTA.
Gold *always* carries ink-black text on it, never white.

### Type
- **Headlines:** Poppins, medium weight, tight tracking (`-0.02em`)
- **Body:** DM Sans
- **Labels/eyebrows:** monospace, uppercase, wide tracking (`0.2em`), small

### Tone
Technical · precise · calm · confident · developer-friendly. A serious
professional tool, not a playful consumer app.

### ❌ Never
- Fake testimonials, fake customer logos, invented company names
- Purple "AI startup" gradients
- Glassmorphism, excessive gradients, heavy 3D
- Cartoon illustrations or mascots
- Invented statistics ("trusted by 10,000 teams")
- Claiming AI features — **MyKavo's detection is deliberately not AI**

## 11. Pacing notes

- **0:00–0:21 is the whole sell.** If the viewer doesn't feel the silent-break
  problem, nothing after it matters. Give it room; resist cutting fast.
- **Scene 5 accelerates**, scene 7 lands still. Contrast carries the ending.
- Motion should be **precise and mechanical** — sharp eases, no bounce, no
  playful overshoot. The product's promise is accuracy; the motion should say so.
- Sound: minimal. A low hum under the problem, one clean tick when the baseline
  is approved, silence under the final tagline.
